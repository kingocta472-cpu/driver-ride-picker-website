1. Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 (RPwD Act)

  • Section 2(ze) - Universal Design: Defines "universal design" as the design of products, environments, programmes, and services to be usable by all people, to the greatest extent possible, without the need for adaptation or specialized design, and applies to assistive devices including advanced technologies for particular groups of persons with disabilities.
  • Section 3 - Equality and Non-Discrimination: Ensures equality and non-discrimination for persons with disabilities, including the provision of additional facilities and benefits such as assistive devices and barrier-free accessibility.
  • Section 40 - Accessibility: Mandates the formulation of rules laying down standards of accessibility for the physical environment, transportation, information and communications, including appropriate technologies and systems.
  • Section 41 - Access to Transport: Ensures access to transport for persons with disabilities.
  • Section 42 - Access to Information and Communication Technology: Ensures that all contents available in audio, print, and electronic media are in accessible format, and that electronic goods and equipment meant for everyday use are available in universal design.

2. Copyright Act, 1957 - Statutory Exceptions

  • Section 52(1)(ab) - Inter-operability: The doing of any act necessary to obtain information essential for operating inter-operability of an independently created computer programme with other programmes by a lawful possessor of a computer programme, provided that such information is not otherwise readily available, does not constitute copyright infringement.
  • Section 52(1)(zb) - Accessible Formats: The adaptation, reproduction, issue of copies, or communication to the public of any work in any accessible format for persons with disabilities does not constitute copyright infringement.

3. Information Technology Act, 2000 - Safe Harbour

  • Section 79 - Exemption from Liability of Intermediary: Provides safe harbour protection, stating that an intermediary shall not be liable for any third-party information, data, or communication link made available or hosted by him, subject to due diligence requirements.

4. Telecom Commercial Communications Customer Preference Regulations, 2018 (TRAI)

  • Accessibility of Telecom Products: Telecommunications products are designed to be accessible to individuals with a broad range of disabilities, and there is a recognized need for mandating international accessibility standards for telecommunication and broadcasting services.

Driver Ride Picker ಸ್ವತಂತ್ರ ಸಹಾಯಕ ತಂತ್ರಜ್ಞಾನ (Assistive Technology) ಯೋಜನೆಯಾಗಿದ್ದು, ದೈಹಿಕ ಮಿತಿಗಳಿರುವ (ಉದಾ: ಕೈ/ಬೆರಳಿನ ಶಸ್ತ್ರಚಿಕಿತ್ಸೆ) ಚಾಲಕರಿಗಾಗಿ ಉದ್ದೇಶಿಸಲಾಗಿದೆ. ಇದು ಯಾವುದೇ ಮೂರನೇ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿಯ ರೈಡ್-ಹೇಲಿಂಗ್ ಪ್ಲಾಟ್‌ಫಾರ್ಮ್‌ನೊಂದಿಗೆ ಸಂಬಂಧ ಹೊಂದಿಲ್ಲ. ಈ ಅಪ್ಲಿಕೇಶನ್ ಬಳಸುವ ಮೂಲಕ, ನೀವು ಯಾವುದೇ ಹಕ್ಕುಗಳು, ನಷ್ಟಗಳು ಅಥವಾ ಹಾನಿಗಳಿಂದ (ಖಾತೆ ನಿಷೇಧಗಳು, ನಿರ್ಬಂಧಗಳು, ತಾತ್ಕಾಲಿಕ ಅಮಾನತು, ಶಾಶ್ವತ ನಿಷೇಧ, ನಿಷ್ಕ್ರಿಯಗೊಳಿಸುವಿಕೆ, ಅಳಿಸುವಿಕೆ ಅಥವಾ ಖಾತೆ ಮುಕ್ತಾಯ ಅಥವಾ ಅಪಘಾತಗಳು ಸೇರಿದಂತೆ) ಡೆವಲಪರ್ಗಳನ್ನು ಸಂರಕ್ಷಿಸಲು (INDEMNIFY AND HOLD HARMLESS) ಒಪ್ಪುತ್ತೀರಿ. ಯಾವುದೇ ಸಂದರ್ಭದಲ್ಲಿ ಡೆವಲಪರ್ಗಳು ನೇರ, ಪರೋಕ್ಷ ಅಥವಾ ಅನುಷಂಗಿಕ ಹಾನಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರರಾಗಿರುವುದಿಲ್ಲ. ಪ್ಲಾಟ್‌ಫಾರ್ಮ್ ನಿಯಮ ಮತ್ತು ಸ್ಥಳೀಯ ಕಾನೂನು ಪಾಲಿಸುವ ಜವಾಬ್ದಾರಿ ನಿಮ್ಮದಾಗಿದೆ.
